Vaibhav Stats

Vaibhav Stats is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @VaibhavBuildsOfficial and the Instagram account @vaibhavbuildsofficial.

It runs on the owner's own computer and reads the owner's own channel statistics (views, watch time, retention, subscribers) with read-only access, so the owner can review how each video performed. It does not post, edit or delete anything, and it is not offered to other users.
